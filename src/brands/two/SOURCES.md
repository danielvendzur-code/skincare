# TWO COSMETICS — source record

Verified: 2026-08-28.

Primary authority is the official TWO COSMETICS catalogue. Product price and availability are volatile; the UI labels the capture date and sends the customer to the official product detail for the current state.

## Official product sources

- HA⁶ HYDRATATION BOOSTER SERUM — https://www.twocosmetics.cz/p/ha6-hydratation-booster-serum
  - verified: 6 forms of hyaluronic acid, gel texture, no added fragrance, all skin types, 50 ml; morning usage is explicit.
- BAKUCHIOL 1 % ANTI-AGE SERUM — https://www.twocosmetics.cz/p/bakuchiol-1-anti-age-serum
  - verified: 1 % bakuchiol, oil texture, no added fragrance, AM + PM usage, official suitability for sensitive skin, 50 ml.
- Hydratačný krém — https://www.twocosmetics.cz/p/hydratacny-krem-s-vitaminom-e-a-bisabololom
  - verified: cream texture, vitamin E, bisabolol, official `pre všetky typy pleti`, 50 ml.
- Krém pre problematickú pleť — https://www.twocosmetics.cz/p/krem-pre-problematicku-plet-s-tea-tree-a-kyselinou-hyaluronovou
  - verified: product/category role for problematic skin, tea tree, hyaluronic acid, cream texture, 50 ml. Medical/therapeutic wording from the source is deliberately not copied into the demo.
- AM/PM ROUTINE CLEANSING GEL 2% SALICYLIC ACID — https://www.twocosmetics.cz/p/am-pm-routine-cleansing-gel-salicylic-acid-cistiaci-gel
  - verified: cleanser, 2 % salicylic acid, gel texture, no added fragrance, 200 ml. Medical/efficacy wording is deliberately excluded.
- Brand/navigation reference — https://www.twocosmetics.cz/ and https://www.twocosmetics.sk/
